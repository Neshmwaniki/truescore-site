The hiring gap

A data hire either compounds your decisions — or quietly corrupts them.

And by the time you find out which, it's months and several reports too late. The usual signals don't tell you who can do the work.

Résumés are marketing

A degree and a tool list don't reveal who can clean messy survey data or design a defensible analysis.

Interviews measure composure

Your senior people burn hours on candidates they can't properly evaluate — and confident talkers slip through.

Generic tests miss the point

Off-the-shelf coding quizzes don't assess research design or M&E reasoning at all — the judgment that actually matters.

Questions

Why not just use a free online test?+

Automated tests check syntax, not judgment. They can't tell you whether a candidate designed a sound analysis, handled messy real-world data well, or would survive a donor audit. That's the gap an expert scorer fills.

Do you place or recruit candidates?+

No. We're independent. You bring the candidates (or your recruiter does); we assess them objectively. No incentive to push anyone through.

How fast is turnaround?+

Typically a few business days from when candidates submit, depending on volume. We agree timelines on the scoping call.

What roles can you assess?+

Data analysts, data scientists, researchers, and M&E officers — junior through senior. If your role lives near these themes, ask on the call.

How do you stop cheating?+

Tasks are designed to reward reasoning over recall, and the scoring looks for evidence of genuine understanding — the kind of work that's hard to fake and easy to probe in a short follow-up.
